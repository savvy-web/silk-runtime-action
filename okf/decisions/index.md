* [A typed CacheKey carries the restore-ladder policy, never a bare string](typed-cache-key-and-restore-ladder.md) - Why the dependency cache key is assembled through the kit's typed CacheKey rather than string concatenation, and the reasoning behind each segment and the two-rung ladder.
* [An embedded, detached Turbo remote-cache server over a Vercel account](embedded-turbo-server.md) - Why the action stands up its own Turbo remote-cache HTTP server rather than requiring Vercel, why it runs detached at a fixed port, and the current-state choices that shape it.
* [BATS helper libraries go to $HOME/.local/share, the one location two consumers agree on](bats-libraries-in-home-local-share.md) - Why bats-core needs no install step, why the four helper libraries land under $HOME/.local/share rather than the tool cache or /usr/lib, and why jq is only ever probed.
* [Effect v4 over the @effected suite, zero @actions/* dependencies](effect-v4-over-effected-zero-actions-deps.md) - Why the action is built on Effect v4 and the first-party @effected suite instead of @actions/*, and why that leaves it with zero @actions/* dependencies.
* [Inputs decoded once, outputs folded once](inputs-decoded-once-outputs-folded-once.md) - One typed Inputs record decoded at the top of the pipeline, and one outputs fold from all-disabled defaults, rather than reads and writes scattered across steps.
* [Kit test layers over hand-rolled mocks, and a real in-memory filesystem](kit-test-layers-and-real-volume.md) - Why unit tests use the kit's partial-override test layers instead of local mocks, why they live in __test__/ rather than beside source, and why the filesystem double is a real volume rather than a stub.
* [Optional work never fails the job](optional-work-never-fails-the-job.md) - Cache restore, Biome/BATS/kcov installs, the turbo cache server and the whole post phase all degrade to a warning rather than failing the workflow, and auto-detected optional work degrades harder than explicitly requested work.
* [Per-step error taxonomy, no central error union](per-step-error-taxonomy.md) - Each pipeline step owns its own Data.TaggedError with a stored message and a reason literal union, rather than a central ActionError type; the standing rules for that shape and its prose.
* [Runtime descriptors are pure, total data — no host reads, no service](descriptors-are-pure-data.md) - Why per-tool install plans are pure functions over an explicit host argument instead of a service, and why Biome and kcov are shaped differently from a runtime.
* [Seams as defaulted parameters, not services](seams-as-defaulted-parameters.md) - Untestable statics (DetachedProcess spawn/awaitReady/reap, process.platform/process.arch reads) are injected as defaulted function parameters, never wrapped in a repository-local service.
* [The pinned package manager leads the child PATH, with no ambient exception](pinned-package-manager-leads-path.md) - Why PackageManagerInstaller replaces corepack entirely, why installPathPrepends puts the manager ahead of every runtime, and why the npm ambient short-circuit was closed.
* [Three pipeline orderings are load-bearing](pipeline-step-ordering.md) - Detection precedes cache restore, kcov is gated on bats actually landing rather than the decision alone, and the turbo cache server starts last — each a deliberate ordering with a specific reason it must not move.
* [Turbo credentials are masked unconditionally, declassified only through Secret.*](turbo-secret-handling.md) - Why every turbo-cache secret is masked before the activation table runs, why the embedded bearer credential is a fresh randomUUID per run, and why a missing token fails the worker closed.
* [Two BATS detection signals, neither a fallback for the other](two-bats-detection-signals.md) - Why bats auto-detection checks both a manifest probe and a bounded glob walk, and why kcov auto follows the bats decision.
* [Two cache entries — the workspace archive and the package-manager store — keyed independently](two-cache-entries-workspace-and-store.md) - Why dependency caching is split into a workspace archive and a store, what each key deliberately omits, and how the store tops up rather than freezing.
* [Windows launches every manager through a shell; POSIX never does](windows-shell-for-cmd-shims.md) - Why shell is true on win32 for all four node-based/bun package managers, the CVE it works around, and the exit-code semantic shift that follows from it.
* [dev/main sync resets by content comparison, never by commit-level history](branch-sync-by-content-not-commits.md) - Why keeping dev even with main compares merged trees rather than commits, why the trigger is a push to main rather than a published release, and why every push is force-with-lease.
* [devEngines-only configuration, no version inputs](dev-engines-only-configuration.md) - Why runtime and package-manager versions come only from package.json devEngines, and why the action's outputs echo the request rather than probe the result.
* [dist/ is committed, bundled and minified, with a mirrored local testing copy](commit-dist-and-local-copy.md) - Why the compiled action ships as committed, minified bundles rather than building at runtime, what the ignore list is for, and why a second, identical copy lives under .github/actions/local.
* [kcov gets its own Actions cache entry, key ladder, and verify probe](separate-kcov-cache-entry.md) - Why kcov is cached separately from the dependency archive, how the ImageOS rung and ImageVersion primary combine to self-heal, and why a restored binary is probed before it is trusted.
* [kcov is built from source because nothing usable is prebuilt](kcov-built-from-source.md) - Why kcov is compiled on every platform, what that build costs, and why it is installed on macOS despite collecting nothing there.
